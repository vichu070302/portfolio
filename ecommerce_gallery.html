Lumina Furniture: Problem Solving & UX Engineering

A comprehensive analysis of how user-centric interface design, performance optimization, and intuitive spatial layouts solved core e-commerce challenges for luxury furniture shopping.

1. The Problem Statement

Traditional furniture e-commerce platforms suffer from high bounce rates due to cluttered product grids, ambiguous dimension visualization, slow mobile page loads, and multi-step checkout friction that disorients buyers.

  • Cluttered mobile navigation disorienting luxury buyers.
  • Heavy unoptimized media causing slow load times (> 4s).
  • High cart abandonment due to complex checkout steps.

2. UX Research & Strategy

Conducted user flow mapping and layout iteration to create a minimalist, dark-mode obsidian aesthetic. Focused on visual hierarchy, instant touch interactions, and streamlined 2-step checkout architecture.

  • Micro-interactions for product material selection.
  • High-contrast dark glassmorphism for luxury appeal.
  • Optimized touch targets for seamless mobile shopping.

3. Solution & Measurable Impact

Engineered a responsive, high-performance web experience with instant dynamic cart updates, crisp typography, and zero layout shift, resulting in higher user engagement and retention.
