UI/UX Case Study & Sensory Design

Perfume Shopping Web UI: Problem Solving & Olfactory UX

An in-depth breakdown of how luxury sensory visual hierarchy, scent note profiling, and dark elegant typography transformed online fragrance shopping into an intuitive digital experience.

1. The Fragrance UX Challenge

Online perfume shopping is inherently non-sensory. Standard web stores fail to convey scent notes, bottle elegance, and fragrance intensity visually, leaving buyers hesitant without physical sampling.

  • Inability to visualize Top, Heart, and Base scent notes.
  • Generic e-commerce cards lacking luxury emotional appeal.
  • Confusing fragrance concentration guides (EDP vs EDT).

2. Sensory UI/UX Strategy

Designed a sensory-driven visual language in Figma. Integrated interactive fragrance pyramids, luxury purple & obsidian accent tokens, and fluid card transitions to evoke fragrance notes digitally.

  • Visual fragrance note pyramids (Top, Heart, Base).
  • Dark mode obsidian luxury theme with gold highlights.
  • Interactive bottle selector and fragrance mood quiz.

3. High-Fidelity Design Impact

Created reusable Figma auto-layout design tokens, interactive prototyping flows, and seamless responsive components, raising design consistency and brand perception.
