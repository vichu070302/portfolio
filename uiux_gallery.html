UI/UX Case Study · Perfume E-Commerce

Luxe Perfume E-Commerce

Designing a premium fragrance shopping experience that helps users discover, understand, and confidently choose a perfume online.

Role: UI/UX Designer Type: E-commerce / UI/UX Design Tools: Figma, FigJam Platform: Responsive Web
OVERVIEW

Introduction & Sensory Challenge

Buying perfume online is different from buying many other products. Users cannot smell or physically experience a fragrance before purchasing it.

The challenge was to design an e-commerce experience that makes perfume discovery simple and engaging while providing enough information for users to make a confident decision. I focused on creating a premium digital experience that combines visual appeal, clear product information, intuitive navigation, and a simple purchasing journey.

THE CHALLENGE

How might we help users choose the right perfume without being able to experience the fragrance physically?

A perfume website can easily become image-heavy and visually attractive, but aesthetics alone are not enough. Users need to understand fragrance notes, scent characteristics, price, and matching preferences.

THE PROBLEM

Key UX Challenges Identified

01 / SENSORY LIMIT

Fragrance is Sensory

Users cannot smell a perfume through a website, so the interface must communicate sillage and notes through visual language.

02 / CHOICE OVERLOAD

Catalog Confusion

Large product options overwhelm users when scent families are not clearly categorized.

03 / TERMINOLOGY

Confusing Scent Notes

Terms like top notes, heart notes, and base notes need intuitive visual representation.

04 / CONFIDENCE

Purchase Assurance

Fragrance purchases require clear pricing, longevity indicators, and ingredient transparency before adding to cart.

05 / SIMPLICITY

Frictionless Flow

Moving from discovery to checkout should require minimal effort and zero cognitive distraction.

TARGET USER

Understanding the Fragrance Shopper

User Goals
  • Find a fragrance matching scent preferences
  • Understand top, heart, and base notes
  • Compare price points & bottle sizes
  • Quickly complete checkout without distractions
User Frustrations
  • Too many similar products without distinctions
  • Unclear scent descriptions & missing notes
  • Complicated multi-step checkout processes
  • Poor mobile view responsiveness
DESIGN RESPONSE

Turning Problems into Solutions

UX Problem Design Response
Users cannot physically experience fragrance Communicate fragrance characteristics through structured note breakdowns
Large product catalogue Clear categories, scent family filtering, and sorting
Fragrance notes can be confusing Present top, heart, and base notes using a clear visual hierarchy
Users need product confidence Give important scent details and reviews a prominent position
Product comparison can be difficult Maintain consistent product-card and pricing patterns
Complex shopping journey Simplify navigation, cart preview, and checkout steps
Different screen sizes Create adaptive responsive layouts across all viewports

REFLECTIONS & TAKEAWAYS

What I Learned

This project helped me understand that good e-commerce UX is not simply about making a website visually attractive. For products that users cannot physically experience, information architecture and content hierarchy become especially important.

"A successful e-commerce interface should not just show users what to buy. It should help them understand why a product is right for them."
